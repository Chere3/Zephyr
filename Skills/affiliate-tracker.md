# 💰 Affiliate Tracker Skill

Track all affiliate programs and commissions in one place. Monitor CTR, conversion rates, earnings, and ROI by platform.

---

## 🎯 What It Does

Central dashboard for affiliate income. Track performance, identify top performers, optimize campaigns, predict monthly earnings.

---

## 🛠️ Features

✅ Multi-platform tracking (Amazon, Stripe, Gumroad, Patreon, custom)  
✅ Performance metrics: CTR, conversion rate, earnings, ROI  
✅ Trend analysis: ↑ growing, ↓ declining, → flat  
✅ Smart alerts: Performance drops, new conversions, goal hits  
✅ Monthly reports + forecasts  

---

## 📊 Metrics Tracked

Per affiliate link:
- **Clicks** — Click-through rate
- **Conversions** — Revenue generated
- **ROI** — Return on investment
- **Trend** — Growth/decline
- **CPA** — Cost per acquisition

---

## 🎯 Smart Alerts

- 📉 Performance drop >20%
- 🟢 New conversion (momentum!)
- 🎯 Monthly target met
- ⚠️ No clicks in 7 days

---

## 📋 Platforms Supported

- Amazon Associates
- Stripe Referral
- Gumroad
- Patreon (monetized)
- Custom affiliate programs
