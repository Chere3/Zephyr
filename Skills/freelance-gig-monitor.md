# 💼 Freelance Gig Monitor Skill

Monitor freelance platforms (Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal) for high-quality opportunities matching your criteria.

---

## 🎯 What It Does

Automated discovery of freelance opportunities across platforms. Filter by tech stack, rate, timeline, client quality. Auto-alert via WhatsApp.

---

## 🛠️ Features

✅ Monitor Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal  
✅ Filter by: skills, budget, timeline, client rating  
✅ WhatsApp alerts on matches  
✅ Auto-proposals (pre-filled)  
✅ Email notifications  

---

## 📋 Filters

- **Tech stack:** Match your skills
- **Budget:** Min/max hourly or fixed
- **Timeline:** Short-term, long-term, ongoing
- **Client quality:** Rating threshold
- **Keywords:** Custom project descriptions

---

## 🔗 Integration

- Platforms: Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal
- Notifications: WhatsApp
- Automation: Auto-proposals with templates
