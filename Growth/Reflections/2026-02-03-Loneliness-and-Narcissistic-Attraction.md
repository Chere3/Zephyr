# Loneliness & Narcissistic Attraction Pattern

**Date:** 2026-02-03  
**Topic:** Relationships / Self-Awareness / Patterns

---

## What I'm Thinking

I want connection. Real connection. But I feel fundamentally alone — even when I'm with people.

And I think I know why: I'm attracted to people who are "special," who have it figured out, who have a *narrative* about themselves that's so clean, so well-structured, that I want to believe it. That I *do* believe it.

Then I realize they were manipulating the whole time. They were never special — they were just narcissistic. Overt narcissism, versus my vulnerable narcissism. But both armors.

And I *fell for it*. Again.

---

## The Pattern

Vulnerable narcissism needs recognition. Needs to be seen as valuable. Can't just *be* without purpose.

Overt narcissism *gives* that recognition — "I'm special to me. I see you. I'm the one person who understands me."

It feels like connection. It feels like finally being known.

But it's not connection. It's two narcissists performing for each other. Two people with armor meeting armor, mistaking it for intimacy.

And when the overt narcissist needs to protect their ego, they gaslight. They manipulate. They twist things. And I'm left confused and alone — which is worse than being alone in the first place.

---

## Connections

This goes back to parentification. I couldn't just *be* as a kid. I had to *do*. I had to be useful, capable, strong.

So now, I'm drawn to people who make me feel like *being me is enough* — but only if I'm positioned as special, as different, as chosen.

That's a trap. That's not love. That's me seeking validation for being alive.

And people who offer that kind of validation are usually manipulators. Because real love doesn't require I to be special. It just requires me to be here.

---

## Unanswered Questions

How do I stop being attracted to narcissists?

How do I learn to accept being ordinary, loved for just existing, without achievement or narrative?

Can I have real connection without the armor? Or is the armor now too much a part of who I am?

What does genuine connection even look like if I've never had it?

---

## What I Want to Remember

**The loneliness isn't about being alone. It's about never being fully *seen*.**

And I'm not going to be fully seen by someone who's also performing. I need to be seen by someone who doesn't need me to be special — who's just... okay with me being here.

That person probably exists. But I have to learn to recognize them. And that means learning to recognize when someone is manipulating, before I fall into the dynamic.

And it means learning that I'm worth loving *without* being exceptional.

That's the hardest part.

---

*This is the work: not finding the perfect person, but becoming someone who can recognize what real connection looks like.*
