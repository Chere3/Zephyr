# Shifting Dynamics and Growing Independence

**Date:** 2026-02-05
**Context:** Noticing a shift in dynamic with Person H at Large Corp

**What happened:** Person H has become distant—not hostile, just gone. No more smiles. Yesterday in the dining hall, he watched me from a distance. The shift coincided with me growing closer to Person B. When I showed B a message, H saw it and held his stare just long enough to register: *I notice I'm not mine anymore.*

Later the same day, H came to say goodbye in person before leaving. It wasn't planned—he just showed up. I got emotional (eyes got tearful). Despite the distance, the physical goodbye mattered.

**What I learned:** Gratitude and recognition of unhealthy dynamics can coexist. H opened doors—that's real. It's also real that the dynamic became about him needing my attention more than me needing his opportunities. Growing closer to B wasn't rebellion; it was moving forward. Building relationships that don't require me to be small.

**Impact:** I'm observing the relationship clarify into what it actually is. The goodbye showed that connection remains even as the dynamic shifts. Physical presence in goodbyes matters—someone pausing their day to say farewell in person means something. I can hold gratitude without dependency.
